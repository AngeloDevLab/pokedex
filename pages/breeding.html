    General Breeding Guide

    Breeding for IVs

    1. Catch or breed a Ditto and Pokémon with good IVs in the stats you care about.
    2. Give one parent a Destiny Knot to inherit 5 random IVs from the two parents instead of the usual 3.
    3. Power items (Power Weight, Power Bracer, Power Belt, Power Lens, Power Band, Power Anklet) force one specific IV to always pass down when held by a parent.
    4. Repeat with the best offspring as new parents to gradually stack perfect IVs across multiple stats.

    Nature Table

    Hidden Ability

    Hidden Abilities are usually passed down from the mother (or from either parent when breeding with Ditto). Common sources: Max Raid Battles, Poké Radar chains, or an Ability Patch/Capsule used on a Pokémon that already has its regular ability.

    Masuda Method

    Breeding two Pokémon from games with different language settings roughly sextuples the shiny odds (further boosted if either parent also holds a Shiny Charm).
